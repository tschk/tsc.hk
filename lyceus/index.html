Lyceus is an experimental transformer family exploring how models can learn from feedback, adapt to a person or domain, and change their own weights and capacity over time.

A research project by The Software Company of Hong Kong.

How it is intended to work

Research prototype

Personal and domain state are distinct from reusable model competence. A host harness is intended to gather observations, use search and approved tools, and turn feedback into candidate changes. Checks decide which changes to keep; retrieval alone does not change the model.

Model sizes

Small research prototypes have been trained and have demonstrated controlled learning and growth. A configurable family and larger models are under investigation; autonomous adaptation and public release remain goals.
